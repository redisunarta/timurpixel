  • Tidak ada akun dan tidak ada server MelekWoy. Data kamu disimpan di HP kamu sendiri.

Izin yang diminta

  • Kamera — untuk foto tempat misi dan misi pagi.

Kamu bisa mengubah izin kapan saja di Pengaturan iPhone.

Pihak ketiga

Apple (pembayaran)

Langganan dibayar lewat App Store. Apple memproses pembayaran; kami tidak menerima data kartu atau pembayaran kamu. Lihat kebijakan privasi Apple.

Meta (iklan)

Aplikasi memakai Meta SDK untuk mengukur iklan: kejadian pemasangan aplikasi, mulai masa coba, dan pembelian (termasuk nilai pembelian dalam rupiah). Meta dapat menerima data perangkat dan, hanya jika kamu mengizinkan pelacakan saat diminta oleh iPhone, pengenal iklan (IDFA). Foto, jam alarm, dan riwayat bangun tidak pernah dikirim ke Meta. Lihat kebijakan privasi Meta.

Menghapus data

  • Pengaturan → Hapus data menghapus foto tempat misi, jam alarm, pengingat, jawaban kuis, riwayat bangun, dan streak dari HP, lalu mematikan alarm.
  • Yang tetap disimpan: tanggal mulai masa coba (di Keychain iPhone), supaya masa coba gratis tidak terulang. Tanggal ini tidak dikirim ke mana pun.
  • Langganan tetap aktif di Apple sampai kamu membatalkannya di Pengaturan iPhone → Apple ID → Langganan.
  • Menghapus aplikasi juga menghapus semua data aplikasi, kecuali tanggal masa coba di Keychain.

Hak kamu

Sesuai UU No. 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Pelindungan Data Pribadi, kamu berhak mengakses, memperbaiki, dan menghapus data pribadi kamu. Karena data Melek Woy! ada di HP kamu, kamu bisa melakukannya langsung di aplikasi. Untuk pertanyaan lain, hubungi kami di email di atas.

Anak-anak

Melek Woy! tidak ditujukan untuk anak di bawah 13 tahun.

Perubahan

Jika kebijakan ini berubah, kami akan memperbarui halaman ini dan tanggal berlakunya.

In short

  • No account and no Melek Woy! server. Your data stays on your own phone.
  • Your mission spot photo is never sent anywhere. Photo matching happens on the phone.
  • You can delete all data at any time: Settings (Pengaturan) → Delete data (Hapus data).

Who we are

Melek Woy! is made by Redi Sunarta (individual developer), Indonesia. Privacy questions: redi.sunarta@gmail.com.

Permissions

  • Alarms — so the alarm rings even in silent mode or Focus.
  • Notifications — one evening reminder before each alarm day.
  • Camera — to photograph the mission spot and do the morning mission.

You can change permissions at any time in iPhone Settings.

Third parties

Apple (payments)

Subscriptions are paid through the App Store. Apple processes payments; we never receive your card or payment details. See Apple's privacy policy.

Meta (advertising)

The app uses the Meta SDK to measure ads: app install, trial start and purchase events (including the purchase value in rupiah). Meta may receive device data and, only if you allow tracking when iPhone asks, the advertising identifier (IDFA). Photos, alarm times and wake-up history are never sent to Meta. See Meta's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → Delete data removes the spot photo, alarm time, reminders, quiz answers, wake-up history and streak from the phone, and turns the alarm off.
  • Kept: the trial start date (in the iPhone Keychain), so the free trial isn't repeated. It is never sent anywhere.
  • Your subscription stays active at Apple until you cancel it in iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Deleting the app also deletes all app data, except the trial date in the Keychain.

Your rights

Under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022) you may access, correct and delete your personal data. Because Melek Woy!'s data is on your phone, you can do this directly in the app. For anything else, email us at the address above.

Children

Melek Woy! is not directed at children under 13.
